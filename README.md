# SalesSight – Power BI E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze e-commerce sales performance, profitability, customer segments, regional trends, and key business KPIs.

## 📊 Project Overview

**SalesSight** provides a centralized view of e-commerce business performance using interactive dashboards and data visualizations. The project focuses on identifying sales trends, analyzing profitability, understanding customer behavior, and comparing regional performance to support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall **sales and profitability performance**
- Track important **business KPIs**
- Identify high-performing and low-performing **products and regions**
- Understand **customer segments and purchasing behavior**
- Analyze **regional sales performance**
- Identify trends and patterns to support business decisions

## 🔍 Key Analysis

- **Sales Performance:** Monitor revenue and sales trends
- **Profitability Analysis:** Evaluate profit and overall business performance
- **Customer Segmentation:** Compare performance across different customer segments
- **Regional Analysis:** Identify high and low-performing regions
- **Product Analysis:** Understand product-level sales and profitability
- **KPI Tracking:** Monitor important business metrics through interactive visuals

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI**
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Measures and calculated metrics
- **Data Visualization** – KPI Cards, Bar Charts, Line Charts, Donut Charts, Tables, and Slicers
- **Business Intelligence & Analytics**

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- Interactive KPI cards
- Sales and profitability analysis
- Customer segment analysis
- Regional performance analysis
- Product performance analysis
- Interactive filters and slicers
- Dynamic charts and visualizations
